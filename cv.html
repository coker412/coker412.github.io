AI and machine learning projects

  • Graph Geometry Research Workspace (AI4M). August 2026–present. Independently designed and open-sourced a workbench for AI-assisted mathematical research, tracking proof attempts, dependencies, and computational evidence. Current explorations include clearer mathematical writing and support for understanding definitions, ideas, and proofs. Public repository.
  • OCT foveal layer localization and thickness measurement. 2026. Second inventor; patent application pending. Designed keypoint detection and boundary refinement for the outer nuclear layer. With model weights fixed, refinement reduced upper-boundary vertical MAE from 0.826 to 0.331 pixels on 119 manually annotated validation images.
  • LLM-assisted dividend forecasting. 2026. Commissioned technical collaboration. Developed a LangGraph workflow combining evidence-linked hypotheses from earnings call transcripts with rule-based, Holt exponential smoothing, and ridge-regression baselines. Collaborated with business analysts on evaluation across 50 companies and iterative improvements.
  • Transfer learning for atrial fibrillation recognition. March 2023–April 2024. Contributed wavelet-based ECG preprocessing and the research report to a project using convolutional networks and transfer learning on MIT-BIH data, adapting single-lead recognition to dual-lead signals.
