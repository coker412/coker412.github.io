I joined the direct-entry PhD program in mathematics at Fudan University in 2025. Before joining Fudan, I studied mathematics at South China Normal University.

My main area is discrete geometric analysis, especially geometric structures on graphs, discrete curvature, and spectral questions related to discrete Einstein metrics. I also work on geometric analysis on manifolds, including volume growth, stochastic processes, and curvature flows.

I also develop AI-assisted tools for mathematical research, writing, and understanding proofs. My applied projects include machine learning for medical images and ECG signals, and LLM-assisted forecasting.

Graph Geometry Research Workspace

Aug 2026–present · open source · AI4M

I built this workbench to organize research in discrete geometry and geometric analysis with Codex. It keeps track of proof attempts, dependencies, and computational evidence so that I can resume a problem without losing its history. The public repository shares the framework and project templates.

I am also exploring how AI can improve the structure and exposition of mathematical papers and help readers understand definitions, ideas, and proofs.

View repository

OCT foveal layer localization

2026 · medical imaging · patent application pending

As the second inventor, I designed a keypoint detection and boundary-refinement method for locating the outer nuclear layer and measuring its thickness. With model weights fixed, refinement reduced upper-boundary vertical MAE from 0.826 to 0.331 pixels on 119 manually annotated validation images.

LLM-assisted dividend forecasting

2026 · commissioned technical collaboration

I developed a LangGraph workflow that extracts evidence-linked hypotheses from earnings call transcripts and combines them with rule-based, Holt exponential smoothing, and ridge-regression baselines. I worked with business analysts on evaluation across 50 companies, case analysis, and iterative improvements.

Transfer learning for atrial fibrillation recognition

Mar 2023–Apr 2024 · ECG signal analysis

The project used convolutional networks and transfer learning to adapt single-lead ECG recognition to dual-lead signals using the MIT-BIH database. My contributions were wavelet-based ECG preprocessing and preparation of the research report.
